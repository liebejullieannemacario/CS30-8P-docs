Bins with fill-level sensors that only alert trucks when full.

Smarter Streets Manila (Full Paper)

Executive Summary

Metro Manila faces persistent traffic congestion and urban management challenges. Traditional infrastructure fixes (such as building more road lanes) are costly and constrained by limited land area. This research paper explores how low-cost optical cameras, smart traffic sensors, and decentralized energy grids provide a sustainable, high-impact alternative for local government units (LGUs).

1. The Problem: Static Signal Cycles

Current traffic lights across Manila operate primarily on fixed timers. A green light stays on for 60 seconds regardless of whether there are 3 cars or 50 cars in the queue. During rainy days or sudden road obstruction events, static timers exacerbate gridlock, resulting in millions of lost productivity hours daily on key corridors such as EDSA, Taft Avenue, and España Boulevard.

2. The Solution: Adaptive Traffic Light Control

By installing camera-based vehicle detection sensors at key intersections, the traffic management system can calculate car density dynamically. When a lane becomes backed up, the system automatically extends its green light duration while maintaining safe pedestrian crossing windows. Simulation models demonstrate an immediate 25% to 35% reduction in average commute wait times.

3. Energy Efficiency & Smart Streetlights

Municipal electric bills consume a huge portion of LGU budgets. Smart LED streetlamps equipped with simple ambient sensors can auto-dim during non-peak midnight hours (11:00 PM – 4:00 AM) and brighten automatically when motion is detected. Integrating solar battery backups ensures intersection lights continue working even during sudden power outages.

4. Addressing Data Privacy Concerns

Public surveillance concerns are mitigated through Edge Computing technology. Visual frames are processed locally inside the camera hardware to output simple numerical counts (e.g., "12 cars in Lane A"). No video streams or face/license plate data are transmitted to external servers or retained in memory, ensuring 100% compliance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012).

Conclusion & Student Reflection

Building a web resource like this landing page helps communicate technical solutions in an accessible, interactive format. Technology shouldn't be intimidating—it should empower citizens and local leaders to make informed choices for a smoother, cleaner Metro Manila.
